Encatch is an AI-powered feedback platform for collecting user input inside your product — on web and mobile — without sending people to an external form.

Most feedback tools link users away from your app. Response rates stay low, and you lose context about what they were doing. Encatch embeds surveys natively in your UI so the experience stays on your domain, matches your design, and captures feedback in the moment.

## Why teams use Encatch

- **Native experience** — Forms render inside your app, not in a separate window or third-party widget.
- **Build once, ship everywhere** — Create a form in the dashboard, then show it on web, React Native, or Flutter after a one-time SDK install.
- **Actionable insights** — AI groups responses by theme, sentiment, and priority so your team sees what needs attention instead of a noisy inbox.
- **Works with your stack** — Route feedback to tools like Jira, Slack, and Salesforce. Self-host if you need to.
